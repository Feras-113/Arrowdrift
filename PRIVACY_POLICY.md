# Privacy Policy – Arrowdrift

_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

Arrowdrift ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game.

**Data stored on your device.** Your progress, coins, items, theme and settings are saved locally on your device only. We do not run any servers and do not collect or receive this data.

**Advertising.** The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use the device's advertising ID and related technical data to show and measure ads, and — where required, such as in the EEA and UK — only after you give consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time under *Settings → Ad privacy settings* (shown where applicable), and you can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Children.** The game is not directed to children under 13 and ads are configured with a PG content-rating cap.
